01 Research

Three threads, one question:
what makes reasoning hold up?

Primary focus

Post-training & reinforcement learning for reasoning

Supervised fine-tuning teaches a model to imitate a solution; it does not teach the model which parts of its own reasoning deserved credit. I work on training signals that operate at the token level — self-play with importance-aware guidance, distillation under transport costs, and RL objectives that trade off reward against drift — so that gains survive outside the training distribution.

  • Token-Level Self-Play NeurIPS 2025
  • MCW-KD AAAI 2026
Current

Coding agents & AI for software engineering

Agents that pass single-issue benchmarks still fall apart over a long-horizon change to a real codebase. I build the benchmarks that expose that gap and the training and inference methods that close it, with security and reliability treated as first-class objectives rather than filters.

  • SWE-EVO Preprint
Foundation

Optimization for generalization

My first research years were spent on the optimization view of generalization: sharpness-aware minimization, multi-objective surrogates, and optimal transport for neural topic models. It is still the lens I bring to post-training — most alignment failures look like optimization failures first.

  • HiCOT ACL 2025
  • SAM for Topic Models NAACL 2025
